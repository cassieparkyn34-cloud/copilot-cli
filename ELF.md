# 🧝 Elf on the Shelf

Welcome to the hidden Elf on the Shelf feature in GitHub Copilot CLI!

## What is this?

The Elf on the Shelf is a delightful Easter egg hidden within the GitHub Copilot CLI. Just like the traditional Elf on the Shelf that appears in different places during the holiday season, our CLI elf brings a touch of seasonal cheer to your terminal.

## How to summon the elf

Launch the CLI with the special `--elf` flag:

```bash
copilot --elf
```

This will display a festive elf-themed banner to brighten your coding sessions!

## About

This playful feature is a nod to the holiday tradition where an elf scout watches over and reports back to Santa. In our version, the elf watches over your code with the same helpful spirit that powers GitHub Copilot CLI!

Happy coding, and may your commits be merry and bright! 🎄✨
