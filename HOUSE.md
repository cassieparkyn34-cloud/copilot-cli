# 🏠 House

Welcome to the hidden House feature in GitHub Copilot CLI!

## What is this?

The House is a delightful Easter egg hidden within the GitHub Copilot CLI. Just like a cozy house provides shelter and comfort, our CLI house brings warmth and a sense of home to your terminal workspace.

## How to display the house

Launch the CLI with the special `--house` flag:

```bash
copilot --house
```

This will display a charming house-themed banner to make your coding environment feel more welcoming!

## About

This playful feature celebrates the idea that your development environment is your digital home. Just as a house provides a foundation and structure, GitHub Copilot CLI provides the foundation for your coding journey with AI-powered assistance!

Happy coding from your new digital home! 🏡✨
